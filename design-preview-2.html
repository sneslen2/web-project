Card layouts & the jump bar

Three card treatments in direction D's palette, then two quick-jump bars. All with real catalog data. The jump bar adapts to the active sort — decades when sorted by year, letters when sorted by title.

Card 2 — The Book Plate

Cover art as a full-bleed banner, faded into the panel with the title plated over it. A corner ribbon carries the state. Handsome and shelf-like, but taller — fewer per screen, and it leans on cover quality (short stories often reuse a collection's cover).

Card 3 — The Index Card densest

No cover art. A library index card: publication year set as a display numeral, title, tags, and a single round check on the right. Roughly twice as many fit per screen, which matters across 282 entries — and it sidesteps the missing-cover problem entirely.
