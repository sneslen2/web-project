Design directions

Each block below is the same screen — navbar, hero with progress, and a row of checklist cards — rendered with real catalog data. D is the current candidate: A's green-and-brass palette blackened, with C's Cormorant type. A, B, and C follow for comparison.

D — The Green Study ← A + C

A's forest-green-and-brass palette rebuilt on a near-black base, with C's Cormorant Garamond display type. Green becomes the mid-tone — the pool of lamplight, the card spines, the surfaces — rather than the whole background, which is what keeps it from reading flat. C's ornamental double border is reduced to a single hairline spine so it stays calm across 282 cards. Deep maroon enters as a third accent, reserved for the in-progress state and the active nav item — so the three reading states each get their own colour: green untouched, maroon reading, brass read.

Cormorant Garamond + Work Sans · near-black, forest green, deep maroon, aged brass
